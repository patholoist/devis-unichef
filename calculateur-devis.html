A Quiet Welcome · 欢迎

An island of warm light & long afternoons.

Shímeí Bay Resort sits on one of Hainan's most protected coastlines — a crescent of soft sand backed by emerald hills, where wild plum trees give the bay its name (石梅, "stone plum").

Forty-two suites and seaview villas, an open-air spa, two restaurants serving Hainanese and Cantonese coastal cuisine, and a swimming pool drawn against the horizon. Days here move slowly, on purpose.

The Bay

Two kilometres of private beach, hammocks strung between coconut palms, and warm shallow waters perfect for swimming from sunrise to dusk.

The Pool

An infinity pool that dissolves into the South China Sea, surrounded by teak loungers, citrus gardens, and a poolside bar serving fresh coconut and lychee cocktails.
